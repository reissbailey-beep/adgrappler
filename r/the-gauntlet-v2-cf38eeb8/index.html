What we spotted

Where enquiries are slipping away

We went through your site on a phone on 2 October. Three spots stood out, and each is exactly what this setup is built for. Orange outlines are ours; tap a screenshot to enlarge it.

1 · Speed to lead

Enquiries wait for a reply

The chat bubble on your site asks people to leave their details, then promises a reply "as soon as we can". Someone messaging at 10pm, or while every coach is on the mat, is left waiting, and in our experience the first gym that answers usually gets the trial.

How the machine fixes it: Gia replies within seconds, day or night, answers the question and offers a trial slot there and then.

2 · Follow-up

No easy way to chase people who go quiet

People can book your £10 trial without leaving a phone number, and afterwards they're told to keep an eye on their inbox. So if someone gets cold feet before their first class, there may be no number for a quick, friendly text, and in our experience that's the text that tends to bring people back.

How the machine fixes it: every enquiry lands in one pipeline with a mobile number, and anyone who goes quiet gets friendly texts until they book or say no thanks.

3 · Friction

Lots of taps between "I'm interested" and a booked trial

Your Instagram bio and Google's "Book online" button both send people to Book Online, where the trials start about one and a half screens down, among 17 options. From your Instagram bio to a paid trial we counted about 8 taps, which is a lot of chances for a nervous beginner to give up.

How the machine fixes it: people tell Gia what they're after, and she books the right trial in the chat in a few messages.

Bonus: quick fixes you can do yourselfFree tips from our check, mostly small jobs in Wix

Things we noticed along the way. These aren't part of what we'd do for you; they're just quick wins for whoever looks after your Wix site.

  • Contact page: "Contact" in your menu opens the Wix template (sample text, a tappable 123-456-7890, info@mysite.com, Wix's own social links). Your real details are already in the footer, so copy them up.
  • Two contact links: the phone menu has "Contact" and "Contact Us", and "Contact Us" goes to the homepage. Delete one.
  • Kids pop-up: the 5 to 7s waiting-list pop-up fills the Kids page about 3 seconds in, for every parent. Open it from the 5 to 7 card instead.
  • Kids "Book A Trial": the button on the 5 to 8 card goes to that template Contact page.
  • Kids trials: the 5 to 7 and Peewee trials show no availability for 6 months, and the 5 to 7 one says "A free trial" but costs £7. Hide them or add dates.
  • 8 to 14 trials: bookable on Tuesdays at 16:15 only (first open slot we found: 13 Oct), while the timetable lists 8 to 14 BJJ Mon, Wed and Fri at 17:00.
  • Age bands and dates: ages differ between the Kids page, timetable and booking page, and the Peewee text still mentions a term "starting in be MAY 2026".
  • Your best page is hidden: /trybjj is excellent but isn't in the menu. Add it, and make it the first link in your Instagram bio.
  • Phone number: make it required on the booking form, and switch on Wix's SMS confirmation and reminder in Booking Settings.
  • Speed: our Lighthouse tests scored the homepage 41 to 51 out of 100 on mobile. Cutting the homepage slideshow to one compressed image would help most.
  • Old offer in an ad: your July ad still said "book before the end of June and get 241". Worth updating before it runs again.
