#1Biggest impactAbout 45 min

Parents looking for kids classes hit dead ends

Kids BJJ is where your champions come from, and parents are careful buyers. Right now a parent on your phone site runs into four things in a row:

On top of that, the 8 to 14 BJJ trial can only be booked on Tuesdays at 16:15, and the first open slot we could find was Tuesday 13 October. Your timetable lists 8 to 14 BJJ on Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 17:00. Ages also differ from page to page (Kids page: 9 to 14 and 5 to 8; timetable and booking: 8 to 14 and 5 to 7; Peewee: "3 & 4", "4 to 5" and "3 to 5").

Why it costs trials: a parent of a 10-year-old sees "waiting list" first and reasonably assumes kids classes are full. A parent of a 6-year-old taps "Book A Trial" and lands on template text. Neither of them rings to ask. They book somewhere that looks simpler.

The fix

  1. In the Wix Editor, open the Kids page, click the waiting-list pop-up (Wix calls it a lightbox) and open its Triggers settings. Turn off automatic display on page load.
  2. Link the 5 to 8 card's button to that pop-up instead, and rename the button "Join the 5 to 7 waiting list". Parents of older kids never see it.
  3. In Booking Services, hide or rename the "Kids BJJ Trial (5 to 7)" and "Peewee" services so nobody can pick a trial that can't be booked. Fix the "A free trial" wording so it matches the £7 price (or make them free).
  4. Open more 8 to 14 trial spots in the classes on your timetable (Mon, Wed, Fri 17:00). If Tuesday 16:15 is a dedicated trial class, say that on the Kids page and timetable.
  5. Pick one set of age bands and use it on the Kids page, timetable and booking page. Update the Peewee text with the next real start date (it also says both "10 weeks" and "12 sessions").

Worth a thought: a full 5 to 7s class with a waiting list is demand you're turning away. If coaches and the upstairs mat allow it, a second 5 to 7s session would move that list onto the mat.

#2High impactAbout 20 min

"Contact" in your main menu opens the Wix template

The same page shows Wix's own Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn and Instagram icons under "Social Media", and a "First Name, Last Name, Email, Message" form. Your real details (020 8998 9262, 07512 098762, reception.gfa@gmail.com) are further down in the footer, so it's not that people can't find you. It's that the first thing they see looks unfinished.

Why it costs trials: people who tap "Contact" usually have a question that's stopping them booking (kids' ages, an injury, "am I too unfit?"). A dummy number that does nothing and a page that reads like a template make a 4.8★ gym look closed. We didn't send a test message, so we can't tell you where that form's messages go. Worth checking.

The fix

  1. In the Wix Editor, open Pages & Menu and go to the Contact page.
  2. Replace the sample paragraph with one real line, for example: "Questions about classes, kids or your first session? Call, WhatsApp or email and one of the team will get back to you."
  3. Change 123-456-7890 to 020 8998 9262 (link it as a phone link), add 07512 098762 as a WhatsApp link, and change info@mysite.com to reception.gfa@gmail.com (link it as an email link).
  4. Click the social icons, choose Set Social Links, and swap Wix's links for your Instagram, Facebook and YouTube (or delete the icons).
  5. Click the form, open its settings and check submissions are emailed to reception.gfa@gmail.com. Send yourself a test.
  6. Back in Pages & Menu, delete the "Contact Us" item that points to the homepage (or point it at the Contact page). Publish, then tap through it on your own phone.
#3High impactAbout 30 min

Nobody who books or asks gets a text

After booking, the page says "keep an eye on your inbox for instructions". The "Get Started" page (linked from your homepage) asks people to "drop us a line" with a form rather than book. So the main ways in either collect an email and wait, or take a booking with no phone number.

Why it costs trials: most first-timers are nervous, and nerves win when nobody checks in. In our experience a friendly text within minutes gets a reply when an email the next day doesn't, and a reminder the day before cuts no-shows. Without a phone number you can't do either.

The fix

  1. In your Wix dashboard go to Booking Settings, then Emails & SMS reminders. Turn on the SMS booking confirmation and an SMS reminder (24 hours before is a good start). Wix's help pages say that once any SMS notification is on, phone becomes required on online bookings automatically.
  2. If you'd rather not use SMS yet, open the booking form and tick Make this a required field on Phone Number anyway.
  3. Change the chat greeting from "We'll reply as soon as we can" to a real promise, such as "We usually reply within 15 minutes, 9am to 9pm". Make WhatsApp the first option if that's where the team actually answers.
  4. Save the three follow-up messages in Section 3 as quick replies in WhatsApp Business, so whoever is on reception can send them in seconds.

Wix sends booking texts from a generic Wix number and needs SMS credit on your plan. Your own WhatsApp is better for conversations; Wix SMS is fine for confirmations and reminders.

#4Medium impactAbout 15 min

Your best trial page is hidden

/trybjj isn't in your menu, and we couldn't find a link to it on your main pages. Your July ad used it. (Instagram hides the other two bio links unless you're logged in, so one of those might point there.) From your Instagram bio to a paid trial we counted about 8 taps (bio link, pick the link, scroll, Book Now, date, time, Next, fill in name, email and age, Book Now, then pay). We stopped before paying.

Why it costs trials: a nervous beginner landing on a list of trials, open mats, day passes and kids options has to work out which one is for them. /trybjj answers that in one screen.

The fix

  1. On Instagram: Edit profile, then Links. Add thegauntletfightacademy.com/trybjj as the first link and title it "Try BJJ: £10 first class, kit lent". Keep Book Online second.
  2. In Wix Pages & Menu, add "Try BJJ" to the main menu, pointing at the trybjj page.
  3. On the homepage, change the BJJ section's "Book Trial" button to go to /trybjj.
  4. Optional: duplicate /trybjj as /trykids for parents, with the 8 to 14 trial and the 5 to 7 waiting list on one page.
#5Lower impactAbout 30 min

The site is slow on phones

Google's PageSpeed site had hit its daily limit when we tried, so we ran Lighthouse (the test behind PageSpeed Insights) ourselves, set up as a mid-range phone on 4G. Three runs on the homepage scored 41, 48 and 51 out of 100, with the main image taking 5.2 to 11.4 seconds to appear and about 3.6 MB to download. /book-online scored 36 and 46.

Why it costs trials: people tapping from Instagram or an ad are on 4G, on the move. Every extra second on a blank screen loses a few of them before they've seen the offer.

The fix

  1. Run pagespeed.web.dev on your homepage to see Google's own numbers.
  2. The slowest single thing is the big background photo in the homepage slideshow. Use one slide, not two, and upload that image at no more than about 1600px wide.
  3. In the Wix dashboard, open Apps and remove any you no longer use. Lighthouse flagged about 460 KB of JavaScript that loads but isn't used on the page.

Honestly, this is the least urgent of the five. Wix sites are rarely fast, and fixes 1 to 4 matter more.

Copy and paste

Three-message follow-up for every enquiry

For anyone who messages, fills in a form or the chat, or starts a booking and doesn't finish. Send from WhatsApp Business or SMS. Swap the {brackets} for real details.

Within 5 minutes
Hi {first name}, it's {your name} at The Gauntlet in Ealing. Thanks for getting in touch! Is this for you or for your child? If it's for you, the easiest start is our Intro to BJJ: 90 minutes, £10, and we lend you a gi. Want me to send you the next few times?
Next day, if no reply
Hi {first name}, {your name} from The Gauntlet again. The next Intro to BJJ sessions are {day, time} and {day, time}. You can grab one here: thegauntletfightacademy.com/trybjj. Any questions at all, just reply to this.
Day 3 or 4, if still no reply
Last one from me, {first name}! If now isn't the right time, no problem at all. If it's nerves, that's normal before a first class, and our coaches go at your pace. Reply "yes" and I'll hold you a spot this week.
